## The architect in a multidisciplinary team

An architect in a delivery team is one of the team, not a reviewer at the end. They:

- work alongside designers, researchers, developers and product managers from discovery onwards
- help the team find what already exists, choose between options and record the decisions as ADRs
- bring the technical constraints into design and research early, and say which can be challenged
- go to show and tells, research playbacks and design critiques, and hear from users directly
- link the team to the platform teams, the solution design authority and the wider [Architecture Community](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/about/architecture-profession/#the-architecture-community)

Not every team has its own architect. Teams without one get support from their solution design authority and the architecture team. Designers and researchers can read [working with architects](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/deliver/working-with-architects/) for when to involve an architect and what to bring.

## How to reach us

- **Questions and suggestions about this site:** [open an issue](https://github.com/DEFRA/architecture/issues)
- **Help designing your service:** the Delivery Architecture team at [delivery.architecture@defra.gov.uk](mailto:delivery.architecture@defra.gov.uk). They help you architect your service, explain governance requirements and tell you the principal architect for your delivery group - see [architecture](https://digital.defra.gov.uk/architecture) in the Defra Digital Service Manual
- **Defra staff:** use the architecture channel on Defra's collaboration tools, or ask your delivery lead to put you in touch
- **Delivery partners:** contact the architecture team through your Defra engagement lead
- **Drop-in sessions:** we run regular architecture clinics for any team, including partners - ask your solution design authority for details

## How we work

- We **work in the open**: this site, our decisions and our guardrails are public by default
- We **engage early** and prefer conversations to documents
- We **measure ourselves** by how quickly teams get to good decisions, not by how many reviews we hold
