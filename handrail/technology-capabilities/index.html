    Technology capabilities¶

    Technology capabilities describe what technology must do to enable Defra's business capabilities, and the strategic option to use first for each.

    Draft - to be confirmed

    Parts of this page, such as names, timings and thresholds, are still being confirmed by the architecture team. Use it as a guide, and check with your solution design authority before relying on the detail.

    Aligned with the government capability model

    Each technology capability shows where it sits in the cross-government Digital Technology Capability Model, from service applications (level 1) to security (level 6). Using the same language as other departments makes it easier to compare, share and reuse.

    Status: Strategic Emerging Gap

    15Strategic
    6Emerging
    3Gap
    Status Meaning What you should do
    Strategic There is an agreed Defra or cross-government answer. Use it. Departing from it needs an ADR and your SDA's agreement.
    Emerging An answer is being established. Talk to the architecture team before choosing, so you can shape it and avoid rework.
    Gap No Defra-wide answer yet. Raise it with the TDA. If several teams need it, we should solve it once.

    Products named here

    Products and platforms are named to help teams find the right people quickly. The capability is what matters - products change over time, and this page will be updated when they do.

    Engage and transact¶

    How users find us, prove who they are, tell us things and pay us.

    TC01 Customer identity and access¶

    Strategic Let citizens, businesses and their agents sign in, prove who they are and act on behalf of an organisation.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • Defra Customer Identity (Defra ID), which uses GOV.UK One Login and Government Gateway as identity providers

    Guardrails: Identity and access

    Supports: 04 Engage with citizens and organisations, 05 Issue licences and permits, 07 Administer funds and grants

    TC02 Staff identity and access¶

    Strategic Single sign-on, role-based access and joiner/mover/leaver for Defra group staff and contractors.

    Government capability model: Level 6: Security

    Use first

    • Microsoft Entra ID

    Guardrails: Identity and access

    Supports: 11 Provide shared services

    TC03 Digital forms and applications¶

    Emerging Capture structured information from users through accessible, GOV.UK design system compliant forms without bespoke front-end builds.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • Defra Forms
    • GOV.UK Forms

    Guardrails: Front end and accessibility

    Supports: 04 Engage with citizens and organisations, 05 Issue licences and permits

    TC04 Notifications¶

    Strategic Send emails, text messages and letters to users.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • GOV.UK Notify

    Supports: 04 Engage with citizens and organisations, 05 Issue licences and permits, 07 Administer funds and grants, 08 Respond to incidents and crises

    TC05 Payments and collections¶

    Strategic Take card and other payments for fees, charges and penalties.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • GOV.UK Pay

    Supports: 05 Issue licences and permits

    TC06 Content and publishing¶

    Strategic Publish guidance, policy, consultations and news.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • GOV.UK publishing
    • Citizen Space (consultations)

    Supports: 03 Create policy and legislation, 04 Engage with citizens and organisations

    TC07 Customer and stakeholder relationship management¶

    Strategic A single view of the customers, organisations and stakeholders we deal with, and our interactions with them.

    Government capability model: Level 2: Corporate services

    Use first

    • Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Power Platform

    Supports: 04 Engage with citizens and organisations

    Operate and decide¶

    How we process work, make decisions and keep records.

    TC08 Case and workflow management¶

    Strategic Route, track and complete work items (applications, inspections, investigations, claims) through configurable workflow.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Power Platform
    • Workflow on the Core Delivery Platform for high-volume digital services

    Supports: 04 Engage with citizens and organisations, 05 Issue licences and permits, 06 Enforce compliance, 07 Administer funds and grants

    TC09 Rules and decisioning¶

    Emerging Express eligibility, risk scoring and business rules as versioned, testable artefacts rather than burying them in code.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • Rules as code libraries within the service (default)

    Supports: 05 Issue licences and permits, 06 Enforce compliance, 07 Administer funds and grants

    TC10 Document and records management¶

    Strategic Store, classify, retain and dispose of documents and records in line with the Public Records Act and retention schedules.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - data and information management

    Use first

    • Microsoft 365 / SharePoint (with retention labels)
    • Cloud object storage for service-generated documents

    Supports: 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence, 03 Create policy and legislation, 05 Issue licences and permits, 06 Enforce compliance, 10 Set organisational direction, 11 Provide shared services

    TC11 Field work and inspection¶

    Gap Plan, schedule and record inspections, surveys and sampling in the field, including offline mobile working and evidence capture.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    No Defra-wide answer yet. If you need this capability, talk to the Technical Design Authority so we solve it once.

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 06 Enforce compliance, 08 Respond to incidents and crises, 09 Operate and deploy assets

    TC12 Incident and emergency management¶

    Gap Detect, log, coordinate and report on incidents such as animal and plant disease outbreaks, floods and pollution events.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    No Defra-wide answer yet. If you need this capability, talk to the Technical Design Authority so we solve it once.

    Supports: 08 Respond to incidents and crises

    TC13 Finance and enterprise resource planning¶

    Strategic General ledger, purchase to pay, order to cash, HR and payroll.

    Government capability model: Level 2: Corporate services

    Use first

    • Departmental ERP (shared services)

    Supports: 07 Administer funds and grants, 09 Operate and deploy assets, 11 Provide shared services

    TC14 Grants and scheme management¶

    Emerging Configure schemes, take applications, manage agreements, verify claims and make and recover payments.

    Government capability model: Level 1: Service applications

    Use first

    • Reusable grants components on the Core Delivery Platform

    Supports: 07 Administer funds and grants

    Data and insight¶

    How we collect, manage, share and learn from data and evidence.

    TC15 Geospatial and location services¶

    Strategic Store, analyse, visualise and serve spatial data - land parcels, boundaries, designations, catchments and observations.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - data and information management

    Use first

    • Defra Data Services Platform
    • Defra Interactive Map, an accessible mapping component (beta)
    • Ordnance Survey data via the Public Sector Geospatial Agreement

    Guardrails: Data

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence, 05 Issue licences and permits, 06 Enforce compliance, 07 Administer funds and grants, 08 Respond to incidents and crises, 09 Operate and deploy assets

    TC16 Data platform and analytics¶

    Emerging Ingest, store, transform and analyse data at scale with governed access for analysts and data scientists.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - data and information management

    Use first

    • Defra strategic data platform

    Guardrails: Data

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence, 03 Create policy and legislation, 06 Enforce compliance, 08 Respond to incidents and crises, 09 Operate and deploy assets, 10 Set organisational direction

    TC17 Reference and master data¶

    Emerging Authoritative, shared records for the things many services depend on: customers, organisations, holdings, land parcels, species, locations.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - data and information management

    Use first

    • Authoritative source per entity - see Defra on a page

    Guardrails: Data

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 05 Issue licences and permits, 06 Enforce compliance, 07 Administer funds and grants, 08 Respond to incidents and crises

    TC18 Environmental monitoring and IoT¶

    Gap Ingest and manage high-volume telemetry and observations from sensors, gauges, samples and earth observation.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - data and information management

    No Defra-wide answer yet. If you need this capability, talk to the Technical Design Authority so we solve it once.

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence, 08 Respond to incidents and crises, 09 Operate and deploy assets

    TC19 Data sharing and publishing¶

    Strategic Share data with partners through APIs and publish open data with good metadata.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - integration

    Use first

    • Defra Data Services Platform
    • data.gov.uk

    Guardrails: Data

    Supports: 01 Act as a custodian of the environment and ecosystems, 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence

    TC20 Artificial intelligence and machine learning¶

    Emerging Train, evaluate, deploy and monitor models, including generative AI, safely and transparently.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - artificial intelligence

    Use first

    • Approved cloud AI services within Defra cloud tenancies

    Guardrails: AI

    Supports: 02 Conduct research and analyse evidence, 03 Create policy and legislation

    Platform and integration¶

    The foundations every service is built and run on.

    TC21 Application hosting and delivery platform¶

    Strategic Build, test, deploy and run services on a secure, observable, paved road with the undifferentiated heavy lifting done once.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - DevOps

    Use first

    • Defra Core Delivery Platform (CDP)

    Guardrails: Hosting and platforms

    Supports: 11 Provide shared services

    TC22 API management and integration¶

    Strategic Publish, secure, discover and manage APIs and asynchronous messaging between services and with partners.

    Government capability model: Level 3: Middleware - integration

    Use first

    • Platform API gateway and messaging on the Core Delivery Platform

    Guardrails: APIs and integration

    Supports: 11 Provide shared services

    TC23 Observability and security monitoring¶

    Strategic Logs, metrics, traces, alerting and protective monitoring feeding the security operations centre.

    Government capability model: Level 6: Security

    Use first

    • Platform observability on the Core Delivery Platform
    • Defra security operations centre (SOC)

    Guardrails: Observability and operations

    Supports: 11 Provide shared services

    TC24 Collaboration and productivity¶

    Strategic Email, documents, meetings and team collaboration for staff.

    Government capability model: Level 4: Foundational technology

    Use first

    • Microsoft 365

    Supports: 03 Create policy and legislation, 10 Set organisational direction, 11 Provide shared services
