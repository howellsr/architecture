# Developing architecture at Defra

<p class="lead">How architects at Defra learn, connect and grow, and how anyone interested in architecture can get involved. This is an early version: we will add to it as the profession develops.</p>

## Architecture roles and skills

Architecture roles in government are described in the Digital, Data and Technology (DDaT) Capability Framework. Use [understand digital and data roles and skills](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/) to see the architecture roles, what each does at each level and the skills they need. It helps you:

- understand what is expected in your role and at the next level
- plan your learning and talk about your development with your line manager
- see how you could move into architecture from another role

The areas Defra architects work in are on [the architecture team](team.md) page.

## The Architecture Community

The Architecture Community brings together architects across Defra, and anyone with an interest in architecture. It runs regular All Architecture meetups online, where people share what they are working on, talk through common challenges and hear from speakers.

You do not need to be an architect to take part. Designers, developers, analysts and delivery partners working on Defra services are welcome.
