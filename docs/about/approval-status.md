# Approval status

<p class="lead">Which parts of this site are agreed, who approved them and when, and which are still draft. Use it to judge how much weight to put on a page.</p>

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Endorsed | Approved by the named board. Teams and suppliers can rely on it. |
| Draft | Published so people can use and improve it, but not yet approved. Check with your solution design authority before relying on the detail. |

Pages still in draft show a "Draft - to be confirmed" banner. Individual guardrails have their own status, shown under each guardrail and in the [guardrail library](../guardrails/library.md).
