# NFR catalogue

<p class="lead">Defra's standard non-functional requirements. Start from these rather than writing your own, so services are consistent and assessors know what good looks like.</p>

**How to use it:** pick your [service tier](service-tiers.md), copy the requirements that apply into your backlog with the target for your tier, and add any service-specific NFRs using the guidance on [writing good NFRs](writing-nfrs.md). Ids such as `NFR-AVL-01` are stable, so you can reference them in stories, test reports and contracts.
