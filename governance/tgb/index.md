# Technology Governance Board (TGB)

<p class="lead">The TGB sets Defra's technology direction. It approves strategies, standards and guardrails, and the investment that shapes Defra's technology estate.</p>

!!! warning "Draft - to be confirmed"
    Parts of this page, such as names, timings and thresholds, are still being confirmed by the architecture team. Use it as a guide, and check with your solution design authority before relying on the detail.



## Purpose

The TGB ensures Defra's digital, data and technology estate supports departmental outcomes, is secure and sustainable, and represents value for money. It is where Defra decides **what** good looks like; the [TDA](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/governance/tda/) and [solution design authorities](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/governance/solution-design-authorities/) make sure it happens.

## What the TGB decides

- Digital, data and technology strategies and roadmaps
- Architecture principles and **Must** [guardrails](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/guardrails/)
- Adoption, change or retirement of strategic platforms and [technology capabilities](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/handrail/technology-capabilities/)
- Changes to level 1 [business capabilities](https://howellsr.github.io/architecture/handrail/business-capabilities/)
- Technology aspects of significant investment cases
- Escalations from the TDA, including risks outside Defra's appetite

## What the TGB does not do

It does not review individual service designs. Those are delegated to the TDA and solution design authorities.

## Membership

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Digital and Information Officer (chair) | Accountable for decisions |
| Chief Technology Officer / Chief Architect | Architecture direction; owner of the guardrails |
| Chief Information Security Officer | Security risk |
| Chief Data Officer | Data strategy and standards |
| Heads of digital delivery and platforms | Delivery and operational impact |
| Arm's length body technology leads | Group-wide alignment |
| Commercial and finance representatives | Commercial and investment implications |

## How it works

- **Meets monthly**, with papers circulated a week in advance.
- Items normally arrive via the **TDA**, which provides a recommendation.
- Decisions, and the reasons for them, are recorded and published here where they are not sensitive.

## Bringing an item to the TGB

Talk to the architecture team first. Most items need a short paper covering: the decision needed, options considered, recommendation, impact on the guardrails and handrail, cost, risk and the TDA's view.
